## Official anchors

- Genocide Convention: adopted 9 December 1948; entered into force 12 January 1951.
- Bosnia and Herzegovina: succession recorded 29 December 1992.
- Former Yugoslavia: the UNTC historical note records signature on 11 December 1948 and ratification on 29 August 1950.
- Application: filed 20 March 1993.
- ICJ judgment: 26 February 2007.
- Jurisdiction: the Court affirmed jurisdiction on the basis of Article IX of the Genocide Convention.

AWS preserves later Federal Republic of Yugoslavia / Serbia accession, reservation, and objections as separate history rather than retroactively rewriting the 1993 dispute.

## Judgment boundary

The judgment contains separate holdings. The canonical authority summary preserves, among other things:

- jurisdiction affirmed under Article IX;
- Serbia not found to have committed genocide through organs/persons engaging its responsibility;
- Serbia found in breach of the obligation to prevent genocide in Srebrenica;
- a breach connected with failure fully to cooperate with the ICTY.

Therefore AWS intentionally does not collapse the entire judgment into a single `PERMITTED` or `PROHIBITED` status.
